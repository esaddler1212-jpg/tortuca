# IRIS daily unlock oath (John’s lantern text)

Displayed and spoken when the Lantern gate loads with `?irisEmbed=1`:

In brightest day, in darkest night,  
I'll embrace my fear, I'll do what's right.  
I choose this ring, I choose this fight,  
In service to this lantern's light.

Voice: browser speech in embed mode (futurist pre-recorded WAV is not used, so words stay in sync).
