# City Nights — first 10 minutes (phone landscape)

**Platform:** phone **landscape** · https://vantagegames1.netlify.app/console/city-nights/

## Minute 0–2

1. **Rotate** phone landscape · **Tap to start**
2. **Skip** Waymo (tap or A)
3. **Pick** starter car (any card)

**Pass:** mph shows · minimap visible · pinned objective mentions **Vallejo taco** or **landmark**

## Minute 2–6

1. Drive **out of garage** on the lit **Sonoma egress** corridor (+Z)
2. Follow **pinned** mission · minimap gold pin
3. Hit **taco truck** then **one landmark**

**Pass:** Bay map unlock toast or “Bay open” mission line

## Minute 6–10

1. Merge **I-80** (east of estate) · hold gas
2. Try **one nitro** (A / nitro pad) or start **ghost sprint** from Missions
3. Optional: **heat** from speed trap — escape once

**Wow candidates:** first freeway burst · Chapter 1 complete · ghost PB toast

## If stuck

- **Garage trap:** update vantagegames1 (maze exclusion + starter road)
- **No roads visible:** Settings → **Lush** or wait for CC0 deploy
- **Portrait:** rotate to landscape (primary target)
